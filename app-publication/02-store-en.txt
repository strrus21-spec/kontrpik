[Full description] (up to 4000 characters)
DotaPick helps Dota 2 players pick a hero against the enemy draft. Mark the heroes the enemy picked and instantly see the best counterpicks based on real match statistics.

Key features:
• Counterpick. Mark enemy heroes, allies and bans. The app shows the heroes that win most often against this draft and fit your team, with their advantage and key items.
• Your position and rank. Picks take your position (Pos 1–5) and rank group into account, from Herald to Immortal, or the pro scene.
• Hero page. Positions, win rate by rank, who the hero counters and who counters him, best teammates and item builds.
• Meta. Tier list by rank and position, weekly trend and the key changes of the latest patch.
• Match review. Paste a match ID or Steam ID to see where the game slipped: lane, fights, vision, and your numbers compared with other players on the same hero.
• Tips. Short animated tricks not everyone knows, plus a quiz.
• For streamers: viewer voting for the pick and an OBS overlay.

To get started, open Counterpick and mark the enemy heroes; recommendations appear right away.

Internet is needed for fresh stats and match reviews. Offline, a built-in copy keeps counterpicks, meta and tips working with the last downloaded stats.
No sign-up. Free, no ads, no purchases. Interface in English and Russian.

Statistics come from open OpenDota data and the Steam Web API and are updated daily.
DotaPick is a fan project and is not affiliated with Valve. Dota 2 is a trademark of Valve Corporation.
